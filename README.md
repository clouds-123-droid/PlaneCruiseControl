# PlaneCruiseControl

## PlaneCruiseControl is a simple Kerbal Space Program mod that helps aircraft maintain a constant speed during flight.

## Features
Toggle Cruise Control on and off with a toolbar button.
Automatically adjusts throttle to maintain the target speed.
Works with most aircraft.
Lightweight and easy to use.
## How to Use
Launch your aircraft.
Reach your desired speed.
Click the PlaneCruiseControl icon.
The mod will automatically adjust throttle to keep that speed.
